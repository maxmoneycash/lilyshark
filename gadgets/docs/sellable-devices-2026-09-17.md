# Which devices gadgets.sh can sell

Updated 2026-09-17 after the catalog expansion. "Sell" means an **affiliate link** (the store pays commission) or the **Buy button** (the buyer pays on gadgets.sh, the maker ships, we keep 5%).

## Headline

The catalog went from 76 to **122 devices**, and affiliate coverage went from 24 devices to **65** — just over half the catalog now sits on a merchant that pays commission.

Lab401 is now the single most valuable application: it pays the best rate (10%) and covers the most devices, because it resells the security hardware whose makers run no programs of their own. Note it **excludes Flipper Zero** from commission.

## Legal note on the security range

The Hak5, O.MG, Proxmark, Chameleon and USBKill entries all carry a plain warning that they are for authorised testing only, and the RFID ones say that cloning credentials you do not own is illegal in most places. Lab401's affiliate terms also **ban black-hat promotional content** (EMV fraud, RF jamming, unauthorized access). Our device pages must stay on the right side of that or the program is lost.
