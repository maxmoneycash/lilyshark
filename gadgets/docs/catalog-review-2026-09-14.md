# Catalog source review — 2026-09-14

## Result

All 44 imported entries reviewed: **41 source-checked, 3 partial reviews**. All 44 have a local source image and credit. LilyShark remains a separate original-hardware concept, with no invented product photograph.

A source check confirms that the retained claims are supported by the linked maker, project documentation or creator listing. It does not establish fulfillment, in-stock availability, final prototype specifications or measured performance. Unknown fields remain explicit even on checked entries. `reviewed` records the editorial pass; `checked` is set only for source-checked records. Prices and availability are dated observations, not live feeds.

## Corrections that affect comparisons

- Kode Dot: replaced the older ESP32-S3 campaign design with the current P4/C5 design and new image.
- CardputerZero: corrected the battery to 1750 mAh and marked work in progress. Mecha Comet now distinguishes its i.MX 8M Plus and i.MX 95 configurations.
- Sensor Watch Pro: replacement watch board, with case and display options separate. Scout Lite and Apex 5: Flipper expansion modules.
- CircuitMess: removed processor and battery claims that the specific current model pages do not establish. Nibble uses AAA alkaline batteries; Artemis Watch 2.0 is advertised ready to use, with inconsistent bundle copy disclosed.
- SenseCAP: T1000-E Meshtastic specifications are separate from the A/B LoRaWAN variants. Radio-range and battery-life claims are attributed or omitted when unsupported.
- New availability filters separate preorder, crowdfunding and development. Gallery prices no longer hide sold-out or preorder status. Wiki pages and comparisons show revision notes and partial-review labels.

## Partial reviews

- **CyperPRO:** original creator campaign identified; final delivered hardware and current availability remain unconfirmed.
- **MAKERphone 2.0:** current campaign identified; final processor, modem, battery and supported cellular bands remain unconfirmed.
- **TICKEY:** live creator campaign confirms the 3.7-inch colour e-paper device; remaining final specifications need stronger documentation.

These entries retain sources and campaign imagery so they can be revisited without presenting original archive specifications as established facts.
