## The proposed job

**Outcome:** give an existing Wio L1 demonstration one concise companion page explaining the selected parts, source-documented setup path and official purchase links for environmental readings on the node's OLED.

**Reader:** a hardware hobbyist following the maker's existing demonstration. The client's actual audience, post and intended placement are still to confirm.

**Selected example:** one Wio Tracker L1 base model with OLED and one Grove BME280. This does not import Pro, Lite or E-Ink accessories into the base-model list. The example is based on maker documentation, not our bench test; no temperature measurements, range, battery life or compatibility result was produced by gadgets.sh.

## Timing, acceptance and payment proposal

**Production:** five working days after complete materials, selected scope, rights and schedule are agreed. Waiting for missing material or client review pauses that production clock. Start date and review dates remain unset. Source contradictions or an unconfirmed software path are resolved or explicitly narrowed before work is accepted; they do not silently become engineering work inside the fee.

**Review:** the named reviewer has five working days to send one consolidated list of deviations from the agreed scope. A real agreement must record the handoff and response dates; this sample does not treat silence as acceptance.

**Acceptance checks:**

- The page has no horizontal overflow at 320px or the agreed desktop width; key controls, local downloads and agreed links work at handoff.
- The parts, quantities, selected hardware/software revision and any offer match the approved source checklist. Remaining unknowns and source-based evidence labels stay visible.
- Creator/maker credit and image/source license information are present; the commissioned relationship is disclosed if the paid work is published.
- The three image sizes, caption, editable page/content files, Markdown reference and dated checklist are delivered. Files open using the supplied handoff instructions.
- The agreed public-origin metadata is delivered; dynamic previews for URL-encoded creator snapshots are outside this static-page scope.

**Corrections:** fix in-scope failures at no extra charge. Include fourteen calendar days of correction for our factual transcription errors or broken delivered-page behavior; set the exact coverage dates in the actual agreement. If a material accepted-scope defect cannot be fixed within five further working days, the proposed remedy is a refund for the undelivered milestone, with its allocation agreed before payment. Sales, traffic, future firmware compatibility and third-party uptime are not acceptance criteria.

**Proposed payments:** $375 after scope agreement to start, and $375 after acceptance. The real parties must confirm invoicing, payment arrangements and the milestone/remedy allocation. Nothing in this sample requests or records payment.
