# First sale: from the demo to a $375 start payment

16 September 2026. Execution proposal only. No prospect has agreed to the $750 pilot, paid a deposit, supplied client media or authorized a commission. No deployment or outreach was performed for this plan.

## The decision

**Show the finished Wio example to three sellers and test one paid use.** Begin with Seeed's mesh campaign team. The remaining question is whether a seller wants this work done for an upcoming post or customer-support use.

A buyer can already publish a page or share its wiki. The fee must pay for useful source reconciliation and finished assets it wants to outsource. The sample demonstrates the format; it establishes neither demand nor reduced support cost.

## What the buyer sees and buys

**Review sequence:** open the public standalone page → inspect the Wio/BME280 example and source limits → download the parts list and one sharing design → read the $750 scope → reply to the actual sender with a use case. The local brief is optional; it exports a document and sends nothing.

The [eighteen-file package](../research/maker-demo-handoff.md) is an independent sample. Its [scope sheet](wio-l1-sample-scope.md) identifies unresolved SKU, power and firmware choices.

**Proposed $750 delivery for one agreed product/build:**

- Editable responsive static page/content files, social metadata for the agreed public origin, and existing merchant checkout links.
- Up to six catalog devices plus stated accessories, quantities, one selected revision/firmware path, prerequisites and up to eight primary sources.
- One approved existing demonstration and up to five supplied images; no new filming or physical reproduction.
- One sharing design in 1080×1350, 1080×1920 and 1200×630, plus a caption, Markdown reference and dated source/link checklist.
- One consolidated review; five working days of production after complete materials and scope agreement. Hosting is separate. The [current scope](revenue-plan-2026-09-16.md#exact-750-pilot-scope) defines acceptance, fourteen days of correction coverage and the bounded remedy.

Proposed payments are **$375 after agreement and $375 after acceptance**. The buyer funds a production asset for its own channels. A sample device, affiliate enrollment or promise to repost does not satisfy the start payment.

## Smallest credible public launch

Publish **the standalone demo's eighteen files** at one owner-approved HTTPS location after final review. Preserve photo attribution and evidence labels. The broader catalog has separate publication work and is unnecessary for this sale.

Add the receiving instruction beside brief exports; prospects can reply to the actual sender. Configure page/image metadata with the real URL and wide PNG, then verify downloads from another device/network. Keep “nothing submitted” status. A localhost address or terminal/ZIP setup is insufficient for a cold prospect.

Use one review page, one human reply channel and the owner's invoice route. Paid traffic, accounts, affiliate activation and analytics implementation are unnecessary for this test.

## One funnel with explicit decisions

These are proposed experiment limits, not conversion benchmarks. Start the clock after the review URL and sending authorization exist.

| Stage | Action and recorded evidence | Go / stop decision |
| --- | --- | --- |
| **Ready to show** | Record URL, reply owner, exact draft and authorization. Cap further speculative preparation at two hours. | Recipient can review and reply without a local server. |
| **Three approaches** | One tailored message per company. Record wording, delivery status and time. | A qualified conversation addresses a real use and commissioning/budget route. Auto-replies, samples or technical answers alone do not qualify. |
| **Ten-working-day review** | Count qualified conversations separately from silence. Follow-ups require authorization and relevant content. | With ≥1 qualified discussion, pursue it. With zero, revise routing/proposition before extending to ten qualified companies total. Silence is not a price rejection. |
| **Scoped decision** | Adapt the scope for the buyer's placement, reviewer, configuration, media, publisher and acceptance terms. | Stop expanding outreach after ten approaches without a substantive conversation. Revise the benefit if three substantive buyers reject it for the same reason. |
| **First cash** | After scope agreement and authorization, issue the $375 invoice. Record receipt date/amount and owner-provided payment confirmation. | **$375 actually received from an unrelated commissioning buyer.** An unpaid invoice does not qualify. Begin custom production only after receipt. |
| **Viability** | Deliver, obtain acceptance, request the remaining $375, record effort and actual buyer use. | Repeat when delivery is accepted and economics work. A deposit alone does not establish profitable fulfillment. |

Use the existing [reply log](first-three-maker-pitches-2026-09-16.md#first-week-manual-outreach-and-reply-log). Record payment evidence and the buyer's actual placement URL or confirmation; email-open estimates add little here.

The model assumes eight production hours plus two sales hours at $50/hour and $25 direct cost: $225 contribution on a paid $750 job. Fifteen hours leaves negative $25. Earlier product-development cost is additional. Count **all** sales effort. Pause at eight production hours if scope is expanding; do not scale two pilots exceeding twelve total hours without revising price/scope.

## Expand only when the first job teaches us something

| Observed first-sale learning | Small next paid experiment |
| --- | --- |
| Buyer uses the page for onboarding or asks to include it with orders. | Quote a revision-specific box insert/support reply pointing to its stable URL. Existing customers supply distribution; no inventory commitment is needed. |
| Firmware changes make an accepted page stale and the buyer requests an update. | Sell one bounded release update with a “what changed” note. Measure that cycle before proposing maintenance. |
| A creator shares the page and another builder documents an adaptation. | Propose one credited original/adaptation pair with approved media and preserved creator links. Extend the [three-build concept](one-board-three-builds-experiment.md) only after actual participation and commissioning interest. |

The larger opportunity is a trustworthy record of which parts and revisions produce a useful result. The first sale should reveal who benefits enough to fund that record, where it gets used, and what it costs to keep correct.
