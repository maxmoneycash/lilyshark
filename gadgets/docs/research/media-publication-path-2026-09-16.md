# Media path for a public gadgets.sh maker demo

Reviewed 16 September 2026. Research and drafts only; no image downloads, permission requests, account actions or publication were performed. `data/images.json` and the existing images remain unchanged.

## The practical first release

**Build the first public pitch around our own interface and two existing Seeed wiki images: Wio Tracker L1 and base XIAO ESP32-S3.** Their exact manifest URLs appear in Seeed’s documentation, and Seeed publishes an explicit license for its own associated documentation images. This is a concrete licensing route, not a claim that the current site has already implemented its conditions.

For a Scout Lite editorial example, replace the present image with a specifically listed PINGEQUA press image. A later commercial portfolio or advertisement should have its intended use confirmed separately. Keep Flipper, LILYGO, Electronic Cats and other storefront photos out of the first public asset export until their applicable permission is recorded.

This does not require waiting for all 66 catalog photographs. Export a small public demo containing only the chosen assets and pages, with the full catalog remaining the local research preview. Present the page as **an independent example by gadgets.sh**, with a separate proposed service offer. There is no commissioned maker, paying customer, sponsor or measured campaign result to imply.

Seeed’s [Documents and Images license](https://wiki.seeedstudio.com/License/) applies CC BY-SA 4.0 to product documents, tutorials and associated images. It separately excludes third-party content, whose own licenses control. The exact URLs below were found in the current page HTML:
