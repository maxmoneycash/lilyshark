# Asset provenance

Product image origins are recorded per device in `../data/images.json` and linked from the corresponding wiki page. All 72 device entries have local images: 44 archive images retrieved on 2026-09-14, 22 additions on 2026-09-15 and six on 2026-09-16 from maker, project or creator campaign sources. The manifest records the source page, downloaded image URL, credit, caption, retrieval date, pixel dimensions and SHA-256 checksum. Each downloaded image was decoded and visually inspected against its device entry. Local copies make this preview independent of third-party image requests. Downloaded files are unmodified; some source URLs request a maker-CDN size. QA contact sheets are separate artifacts. No AI product images were generated. LilyShark remains a typographic concept placeholder.

These images are reference material for this local preview. Linking to an origin does not grant a commercial reuse license. Review each owner's image-use terms or replace with licensed/original photography before publishing the site. No maker endorsement is implied.

Model-specific image notes: Kode Dot uses its current proposed design; Flipper One, CardputerZero, HighBoy and POOM use labeled development illustrations. CyperPRO and TICKEY retain campaign images. M1 uses an older maker image with a revision caveat. Sensor Watch Pro shows a watch case that is sold separately; CircuitPet uses the original maker anatomy photograph. ByteBoi 2.0 and Artemis Watch 2.0 now use their specific 2.0 galleries.

The creator redesign also uses locally hosted variable Bricolage Grotesque, from the official Google Fonts repository (`ofl/bricolagegrotesque`), with its SIL Open Font License in `public/assets/BricolageGrotesque-OFL.txt`. Display type uses Bricolage; body text retains Instrument Sans. Creator-supplied photo URLs are separate from the researched catalog assets.

The redesign uses locally hosted Instrument Sans (400, 600 and 700), obtained from the Google Fonts distribution on 2026-09-14. It uses the SIL Open Font License, included as `public/assets/InstrumentSans-OFL.txt`. The earlier Big Shoulders Display and Spline Sans Mono assets and their licenses remain in the asset directory but are no longer loaded by the site.

The favicon and the deliberately typographic placeholders were created for this app. A placeholder is labeled as missing photography or a concept, rather than being presented as a photo of existing hardware.

The hardware carousel uses the local Inter font and React/Motion runtime from the user-supplied `website-cloner/sites/vertical-carousel-3d/source` reference. See [carousel provenance and adaptations](hardware-carousel.md). The carousel uses the reference’s full-bleed cover crop; device detail pages retain the full product images. No demo lifestyle photographs were copied into the hardware catalog.
